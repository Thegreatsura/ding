# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to contribute to Ding. Treat people with respect, give feedback on the work, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone’s private information without consent are unacceptable. These standards apply in Ding’s issue tracker, pull requests, and other project spaces.

## Reporting and moderation

Report concerns privately using the maintainer’s [contact details](https://yusufcengiz.dev/). Include the relevant links and describe what happened. You can also [report abuse directly to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The maintainer will handle reports privately and share details only as needed to review and respond. Depending on the circumstances, the maintainer may remove content, give a warning, or restrict participation.
